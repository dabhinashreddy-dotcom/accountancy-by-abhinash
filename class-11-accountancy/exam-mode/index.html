Class 11 Accountancy Exam Mode — Timed Mock Exams with Certificates

Practise real exam conditions: a timer, mixed questions across all 18 chapters or a single chapter, instant scoring, and a downloadable certificate when you finish. Every mistake is saved to your Mistake Book for targeted revision.

What Happens in Exam Mode

Choose your chapters, question count and time limit. The exam draws questions round-robin across chapters so no topic dominates. Navigation is logged while the timer runs, answers are locked when time expires, and the result screen explains every question. Your score, time used and certificate are stored on your device — nothing is uploaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Exam Mode like the real board exam?

It mimics the pressure — timed, mixed-syllabus, auto-scored — but the question style is this site's own practice material, not actual CBSE board papers.

Can I retake an exam?

Yes, as many times as you like. Repeating a mixed exam after revising your Mistake Book is one of the most effective ways to raise your score.
