## Unit 1 — Accounting for Partnership Firms (36 marks)

- [ ] Partnership: features, Partnership Deed
- [ ] Provisions of the Indian Partnership Act 1932 in absence of partnership deed
- [ ] Fixed v/s fluctuating capital accounts
- [ ] Profit & Loss Appropriation Account — division of profit among partners, guarantee of profits
- [ ] Past adjustments (interest on capital, interest on drawings, salary, profit sharing ratio)
- [ ] Note: Interest on partner's loan = charge against profits
- [ ] Goodwill: meaning, nature, factors affecting, valuation methods — average profit, super profit, capitalisation
- [ ] Change in profit-sharing ratio among existing partners: sacrificing ratio, gaining ratio, revaluation of assets, reassessment of liabilities, treatment of reserves, accumulated profits and losses; preparation of revaluation account and revised balance sheet
- [ ] Admission of a partner: effect on PSR, goodwill treatment as per AS-26, revaluation, reserves/accumulated profits/losses, capital adjustment, revised balance sheet
- [ ] Retirement and death of a partner: effect on PSR, gaining ratio, goodwill (AS-26), revaluation, reserves/accumulated profits/losses, partners' capital & current accounts, revised balance sheet, retiring partner's loan account, deceased partner's share of profit till date of death, deceased partner's capital account, executor's account
- [ ] Dissolution of a partnership firm: meaning of dissolution of partnership and dissolution of firm, types of dissolution, settlement of accounts — realisation account, partners' capital accounts, cash/bank account
  - EXCLUDED: piecemeal distribution, sale to a company, insolvency of partner(s)
  - Notes: tangible asset realised value not given → book value; intangible not given → nil; realization expenses borne by partner → indication of payment

## Unit 2 — Accounting for Companies (24 marks)

- [ ] Features and types of companies
- [ ] Share and share capital: nature and types
- [ ] Issue and allotment of equity and preference shares; public subscription; over/under subscription; issue at par and premium
- [ ] Calls in advance and calls in arrears (EXCLUDING interest)
- [ ] Issue of shares for consideration other than cash
- [ ] Private placement, Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP), sweat equity — concepts
- [ ] Forfeiture and reissue of shares
- [ ] Disclosure of share capital in Balance Sheet (Schedule III Part I, Companies Act 2013)
- [ ] Debentures: meaning, types
- [ ] Issue of debentures at par, at premium, at discount
- [ ] Issue of debentures for consideration other than cash
- [ ] Issue of debentures with terms of redemption (issue-related terms only)
- [ ] Debentures as collateral security (concept)
- [ ] Interest on debentures (EXCLUDING TDS concept)
- [ ] Writing off discount / loss on issue of debentures — from Security Premium Reserve (if any), then Statement of Profit and Loss as financial cost (AS-16)
  - EXCLUDED: full redemption accounting (payment/purchase in open market/conversion entries) as standalone prescribed topic

## Option 1 Part B — Financial Statement Analysis (20 marks)

### Unit 3: Analysis of Financial Statements (12 marks)
- [ ] Financial statements of a company: meaning, nature, uses and importance
- [ ] Statement of Profit and Loss and Balance Sheet — Schedule III to Companies Act 2013 major headings and sub-headings
  - EXCLUDED: exceptional items, extraordinary items, profit (loss) from discontinued operations
- [ ] Financial statement analysis: meaning, significance, objectives, importance, limitations
- [ ] Tools: comparative statements, common-size statements, ratio analysis, cash flow analysis
- [ ] Accounting ratios: meaning, objectives, advantages, classification, computation
  - Liquidity: Current ratio, Quick ratio
  - Solvency: Debt-to-Equity, Total Assets-to-Debt, Proprietary, Interest Coverage, Debt-to-Capital-Employed
  - Activity: Inventory Turnover, Trade Receivables Turnover, Trade Payables Turnover, Fixed Asset Turnover, Net Asset Turnover, Working Capital Turnover
  - Profitability: Gross Profit, Operating, Operating Profit, Net Profit, Return on Investment
  - Note: Net Profit Ratio computed on profit before AND after tax

### Unit 4: Cash Flow Statement (8 marks)
- [ ] Meaning, objectives, benefits of cash flow information
- [ ] Cash and cash equivalents
- [ ] Operating, investing, financing activities
- [ ] Preparation of Cash Flow Statement — indirect method only, AS-3 (Revised)
- [ ] Adjustments: depreciation & amortisation, profit/loss on sale of assets including investments, dividend (final & interim), tax
- [ ] Note (i): bank overdraft & cash credit treated as short-term borrowings
- [ ] Note (ii): current investments treated as marketable securities unless otherwise stated
- [ ] Note (iii): previous years' proposed dividend as per AS-4; current years' proposed dividend accounted in next year after declaration

### Part C: Project Work (20 marks — Project File 12 + Viva Voce 8)
- [ ] One project on financial statement analysis of a company, any two aspects:
  comparative and common size financial statements, accounting ratios, segment reports, cash flow statements

## Option 2 Part B — Computerised Accounting (20 marks)

### Unit 4: Computerised Accounting
- [ ] Overview of CAS: introduction and application in accounting; features of CAS; structure of CAS
- [ ] Software packages: generic, specific, tailored
- [ ] Electronic spreadsheet: concept and features
- [ ] Spreadsheet applications for accounting information: bank reconciliation, asset accounting, loan repayment schedules, ratio analysis
- [ ] Data representation: graphs, charts and diagrams
- [ ] Steps in installation and setup of CAS
- [ ] Codification and hierarchy of account heads; creation of accounts
- [ ] Data entry, validation and verification
- [ ] Adjusting entries; preparation of statements with closing and opening entries
- [ ] System security features (need and security of the system)
